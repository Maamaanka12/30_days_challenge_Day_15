Do I need to be home while you clean?+

Not at all. Most customers are out at work or running errands. You can leave access instructions in your booking, and we'll send a photo when we've finished.

What products do you use?+

Plant-based, biodegradable products in refillable bottles. They're safe for kids, pets and most surfaces. Prefer your own products? Just let us know.

Can I reschedule or cancel?+

Yes — up to 24 hours before your visit, at no charge. Move a clean with one click in your account, or reply to your booking email.

How do I know I can trust my cleaner?+

Every team member is background-checked, insured and trained in our checklist. You'll get the same cleaner whenever possible, and we're one message away if anything's off.
